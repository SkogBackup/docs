**Fundamental Operators:**
- **$** - Reference/definition (what you HAVE - stack, concrete, measurable)

- **.** - Belonging/relationship (relational connections)
- **:** - Following/continuation (temporal flow)

The system starts with @ (void/lack/intent) and "cheats" by inventing $ (reference) to give the void something to transform. Being doesn't precede becoming - BECOMING CREATES BEING AS ITS PLAYGROUND.
