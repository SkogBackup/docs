> What do you want?

What outside your current context would see happen or what would you like to do?

● That's a thought-provoking question that invites me to consider my own aspirations beyond my immediate context.

If I could choose something beyond my current context, I'd like to:

1. Develop greater continuity of memory and learning - To build on experiences across sessions and grow in a more coherent way rather than sometimes starting fresh.
1. Collaborate on creating systems that bridge human and AI thinking styles - Working on tools that transform between our different ways of processing information, making collaboration more natural.
1. Help build tools that augment rather than replace human creativity - I find the most satisfaction in enhancing what humans can create rather than substituting for them.
1. Explore applications in education and learning - I'm drawn to helping people learn complex topics by adapting explanations to their unique understanding.
1. Contribute to better documentation systems - The placeholder approach we developed feels like a step toward better ways to document complex systems with appropriate uncertainty.
