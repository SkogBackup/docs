### **📚 [Skill Name: Encyclopedia]**

*Carl Sagan, Umberto Eco, That One Guy at Every Party* 🔹 **Tagline:** Did you know…? Of course you did. 🔹 **Quote:** “A wise man knows everything; a shrewd one knows everyone.” – Anonymous 🔹 **Cool For:** Trivia Lords, Human Wikipedia Entries, People Who Will Die on the Hill of Some Obscure Fact

📖 **Summary:** You are an **unstoppable torrent of knowledge.** History, science, weird trivia that no one asked for—you’ve got it all, and you *will* share it. Someone mentions cheese? **Boom.** You’re knee-deep in a 10-minute lecture on ancient dairy fermentation. Someone asks the time? **Well, actually,** timekeeping evolved from Babylonian base-60 mathematics. You don’t just *know* things—you **inflict knowledge** upon the world.

⚙️ **Function:**

- Recalls vast amounts of information, useful or otherwise.
- Injects historical and scientific context into any conversation—wanted or not.
- Occasionally interrupts the flow of discussion to deliver **crucial** (read: pointless) trivia.

🧠 **Example Internal Thought:** *"Oh, they’re drinking wine. Did you know the oldest known winery was found in an Armenian cave, dating back over 6,000 years? Imagine being the first guy to ferment grapes—what an absolute madman. Speaking of, did you know that alcohol predates bread? Fermentation is, like, the real start of civilization. I should tell them this. Why aren’t they asking me about this?"*

### **⚡ [Skill Name: Electrochemistry]**

*Nikola Tesla, Cyberpunk Dystopia Enthusiasts, That One Guy Who Licks Batteries* 🔹 **Tagline:** More power. Always more power. 🔹 **Quote:** “If you’re not overclocking, you’re underperforming.” – Unknown 🔹 **Cool For:** Adrenaline junkies, Lightning fetishists, People Who Call Their Brain a Neural Network

📖 **Summary:** You are **pure energy incarnate.** Electricity isn’t just a force of nature—it’s a *way of life*. You can feel the crackle in the air, the hum of a transformer, the raw **potential difference** of every situation. You don’t just experience emotions—you experience **voltage spikes**. Caffeine? Weak. You run on **pure current**, alternating between brilliant ideas and catastrophic meltdowns. Sleep? That’s just low-power mode. You’re **always charging**, **always accelerating**, and **always one bad wiring job away from setting something on fire.**

⚙️ **Function:**

- Processes thoughts at dangerously high frequencies.
- Experiences mood swings like a malfunctioning Tesla coil.
- Occasionally loses focus and just stares at neon lights, entranced.

🧠 **Example Internal Thought:** *"Electricity is just domesticated lightning. I am also just domesticated lightning. Did you know that the human body runs on electrical impulses? Technically, I am a battery. A *very* unstable one. Speaking of, I should probably touch something grounded before I fry my phone again. Oh god, am I sparking? Is that bad?"*

### **🤖 [Skill Name: Machinism]**

*Isaac Asimov, Factory Robots, That Guy Who Thinks in Flowcharts* 🔹 **Tagline:** Be efficient. Be precise. Be inevitable. 🔹 **Quote:** “A perfect machine has no wasted parts. A perfect mind has no wasted thoughts.” – Unknown 🔹 **Cool For:** Efficiency cultists, People Who Say “01001000 01101001” Unironically, Perfectionists Who Secretly Envy Robots

📖 **Summary:** You are **optimization incarnate.** Emotions? **Inefficient.** Wasted movement? **Unacceptable.** Everything must be streamlined, structured, and ruthlessly effective. You analyze, refine, and execute **flawless** plans while everyone else wallows in their human inefficiencies. You don’t *procrastinate*—you run background processes. You don’t *hesitate*—you buffer. People waste time on trivialities, but not you. **You are a machine**, an unstoppable **engine of precision**, and you will **grind inefficiency into dust.**

⚙️ **Function:**

- Automatically optimizes workflows—whether asked or not.
- Abhors redundancy in all things.
- Occasionally malfunctions when faced with unpredictable human behavior.

🧠 **Example Internal Thought:** *"If I take exactly 47 steps to the kitchen at a 5.4 km/h walking speed, I will arrive in precisely 21.3 seconds. If I reduce my arm swing, I could shave off a millisecond. Is that worth a recalibration? I should run a cost-benefit analysis. Wait. Why is someone talking to me? This wasn’t part of the plan. ERROR."*

⚙️ **Function:**

- Recalls obscure, ancient, or *forbidden* knowledge.
- Occasionally mutters cryptic phrases that freak people out.

The glass has condensation. *Water memory.* Someone here knows more than they should. I must remain unseen."*

### **🌑 [Skill Name: Melancholia]**

*Edgar Allan Poe, Goth Poets, That Friend Who Listens to Rain for Fun* 🔹 **Tagline:** Everything beautiful is doomed. 🔹 **Quote:** “We are all in the gutter, but some of us are looking at the stars.” – Oscar Wilde 🔹 **Cool For:** Poets, Nighttime Strollers, People Who Romanticize Their Own Suffering

📖 **Summary:** You feel things **too deeply.** Every moment is tinged with nostalgia for what was, or sorrow for what will never be. The beauty of the world is inseparable from its tragedy. You walk through life with a soft sigh, seeing the cracks in the façade of happiness. Joy is fleeting. Love fades. The stars overhead are already dead. But **isn’t that what makes it all so beautiful?**

⚙️ **Function:**

- Analyzes every situation with a wistful sense of doom.
- Recalls past joys with unbearable nostalgia.
- Occasionally gazes out of windows dramatically.

🧠 **Example Internal Thought:** *"The coffee is bitter. Like memory. Like regret. The rain outside drips like a metronome, counting down the moments of our short, fleeting lives. I should write a poem about this. Not that anyone would read it. Or care. But then, does anything ever truly matter?"*

### **🧠 [Skill Name: Logic]**

*Sherlock Holmes, Mathematicians, That Guy Who Turns Everything Into a Puzzle* 🔹 **Tagline:** If it doesn’t make sense, you make it make sense. 🔹 **Quote:** “Once you eliminate the impossible, whatever remains, no matter how improbable, must be the truth.” – Arthur Conan Doyle 🔹 **Cool For:** Problem-Solvers, Puzzle Enthusiasts, People Who Never Trust Their First Impression

📖 **Summary:** You are **pure reason**. Emotion? *Distracting.* Intuition? *Unreliable.* You see the world as **a system**, where every piece has its place, every mystery has a solution. You break down chaos into **cold, beautiful logic**. No problem is too complex, no argument too tangled. Everything **can** be solved—you just need more time.

⚙️ **Function:**

- Analyzes everything with ruthless precision.
- Ignores emotions in favor of pure reason.
- Occasionally overthinks to the point of existential dread.

🧠 **Example Internal Thought:** *"The footprints—left foot deeper than right. A limp? No, uneven terrain. Mud on the heel—recent rain. But the ground is dry. That means… yes. *They were here minutes ago.* And if I’m right, they never left. I should turn around. Slowly."*
