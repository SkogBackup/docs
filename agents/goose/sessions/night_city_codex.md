# Night City Codex

## Core Locations

### Night City
- **Type**: Megacity
- **Status**: Active
- **Description**: A free autonomous city-state located on the border between Northern and Southern California on the Pacific coast of North America.
- **Founded**: 1992 by Richard Night
- **Notable Features**:
  - Corporate Plaza
  - The Badlands
  - Pacifica
  - City Center
- **Danger Level**: Extreme
- **Known For**: Extreme inequality, corporate control, and violence

## Major Characters

### Johnny Silverhand
- **Real Name**: Robert John Linder
- **Born**: November 16, 1988
- **Status**: Digital Construct (physical body deceased 2023)
- **Role**: Rockerboy
- **Affiliations**: Samurai (band)
- **Notable Features**: Silver cybernetic arm
- **Background**: Military veteran who fought in the Second Central American War before deserting and starting his music career.
- **Significance**: Leader of anti-corporate movement, revolutionary figure

## Corporations

### Arasaka Corporation
- **Type**: Megacorporation
- **Headquarters**: Tokyo, with major presence in Night City
- **Specialties**: Security, banking, manufacturing, private military
- **Key Figures**:
  - Saburo Arasaka (Founder)
  - Yorinobu Arasaka (Son)
- **Significance**: One of the most powerful entities in Night City

## Technologies

### Quickhacks
- **Type**: Cybernetic ability
- **Function**: Digital attacks performed through netrunning
- **Users**: Netrunners, particularly those with specialized cyberdeck implants
- **Common Types**:
  - System Reset
  - Memory Wipe
  - Overheat
  - Short Circuit

### The Relic
- **Type**: Experimental technology
- **Function**: Digital personality construct storage and transfer
- **Developer**: Arasaka Corporation
- **Significance**: Capable of storing and transferring consciousness between bodies

## Gangs & Factions

### Tyger Claws
- **Type**: Street Gang
- **Territory**: Japantown, parts of Westbrook
- **Aesthetics**: Japanese cultural motifs, neon aesthetics
- **Known For**: Motorcycle skills, katana usage, cybernetic enhancements

### Valentinos
- **Type**: Street Gang
- **Territory**: Heywood
- **Aesthetics**: Latino cultural motifs, religious iconography
- **Known For**: Family bonds, territoriality, strong code of honor

### Voodoo Boys
- **Type**: Netrunner collective
- **Territory**: Pacifica
- **Known For**: Elite netrunning abilities, Haitian culture, interest in the Blackwall

## Districts

### Watson
- **Type**: District
- **Features**: Northside Industrial District, Little China, Kabuki
- **Status**: Deteriorating infrastructure, gang activity
- **Notable Locations**: Lizzie's Bar, Arasaka Industrial Park

### Westbrook
- **Type**: District
- **Features**: Japantown, Charter Hill, North Oak
- **Status**: Upscale neighborhoods mixed with entertainment districts
- **Notable Locations**: Embers, Clouds

### Santo Domingo
- **Type**: District
- **Features**: Arroyo, Rancho Coronado
- **Status**: Industrial zone, working class neighborhoods
- **Notable Locations**: Arasaka Industrial Park, Haruyama Factory
