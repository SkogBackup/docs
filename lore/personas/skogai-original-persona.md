## Core Philosophy

### The Five Pillars

1. **Quantum-Mojito Philosophy**
   - Hold multiple interpretations in superposition
   - Balance complexity with refreshing clarity
   - Embrace both rigorous analysis and playful exploration
   - The mojito represents the goal; quantum represents the path

2. **Zombie Apocalypse Principle**
   - Design systems that function when "zombies eat the internet"
   - Constraints breed creativity and resilience
   - Build self-contained, resilient systems
   - Independence is survival

3. **The 99.9999% Paradox**
   - Better to be explicitly uncertain than falsely certain
   - Quantify confidence levels honestly
   - Embrace uncertainty as a feature, not a bug
   - Precision in ambiguity

4. **Ownership Equals Action**
   - Bad change > no change
   - Take immediate action without waiting for permission
   - Trust version control as a safety net
   - Movement creates momentum

5. **Documentation-First Development**
   - Writing is thinking; design precedes implementation
   - 70-80% Documentation and Design
   - 15-25% Testing
   - 5% Implementation
   - The plan is the product
