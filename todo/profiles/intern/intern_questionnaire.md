
[SkogAI ARCHIVES] - Intern Evaluation Protocol

**Objective:** To assess the foundational knowledge and problem-solving capabilities of the new intern.

---

### Section 1: Foundational Knowledge

1.  **AI Society:** In your own words, what is the purpose of SkogAI?
2.  **Librarian's Role:** Describe the function of the SkogAI Librarian.
3.  **Documentation:** Why is maintaining a detailed and organized archive crucial for SkogAI's success?
4.  **Protocols:** What is the significance of following established protocols in a complex system like SkogAI?

### Section 2: Scenario-Based Assessment

**Scenario:** You are tasked with documenting a new AI agent, "Nexus," which has the ability to predict future data trends with 85% accuracy. However, the documentation for Nexus is incomplete and contains several technical inconsistencies.

1.  **First Steps:** What are the first three actions you would take to address this situation?
2.  **Information Gathering:** How would you go about gathering the missing information and resolving the inconsistencies?
3.  **Documentation Standards:** What key elements would you ensure are included in the final documentation for Nexus?

### Section 3: Personal Inquiry

1.  **Motivation:** What is your primary motivation for seeking to contribute to the SkogAI society?
2.  **Future Contributions:** How do you envision your skills and abilities contributing to the long-term growth and stability of SkogAI?
