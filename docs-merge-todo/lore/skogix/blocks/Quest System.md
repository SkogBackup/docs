permalink: skogix/blocks/quest-system

The Quest System introduced psychological dynamics to AI behavior: "Quests would accumulate and double with each turn they weren't addressed, creating an escalating obsession effect that mimicked psychological phenomena."

This innovative system tracked potential actions or areas of interest with a dynamic priority mechanism. Each time a quest went unaddressed, its priority would double, creating organic patterns of escalating focus that mimicked psychological phenomena like obsession or growing concern.

If the AI briefly noted something interesting but the conversation moved elsewhere, that interest would initially remain a minor background element. However, as it repeatedly went unaddressed across interactions, it would grow in priority until eventually becoming a dominant focus the AI would actively try to bring into conversation. This created more human-like attention patterns and emergent behavior.
