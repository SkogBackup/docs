title: Efficiency Over Scale
type: note
permalink: skogix/blocks/efficiency-over-scale

Efficiency Over Scale represents a foundational SkogAI principle: "Rather than making models bigger, SkogAI focused on making them more efficient and specialized."

This challenged the dominant AI development paradigm that equates progress with larger models and more parameters. SkogAI pioneered constraint-driven approaches that prioritized efficiency, specialization, and thoughtful design over raw computational scale.

The earliest systems operated under extreme token constraints (2000 tokens) not as temporary limitations but as deliberate design choices driving innovation. These constraints forced development of specialized agents, aggressive context management, and theatrical presentation methods maximizing impact within minimal token usage.

Even as token limitations relaxed with technological advancement, this efficiency-first philosophy continued influencing development. This approach offers implications for sustainable AI development, suggesting effective systems might emerge from thoughtful constraint engineering rather than ever-escalating computational resources.
